Are You Deployed?

If you would like to receive a care package, please email civiliansupplycorps@gmail.com with the following information:

  • Name and APO/FPO address
  • Expected date of return
  • Is the box just for you or to share? (How many in unit?)
  • Unit nickname
  • Any special requests?

Items We Often Have:

  • Books
  • Hygiene items (wipes, shampoo, toothbrush, etc.)
  • OTC medicine (Tylenol, acid reflux tabs, vitamins)
  • Snacks (cookies, crackers, jerky, drink mix, candy)

Items We Can Get:

  • Anything that passes customs
  • Extra clothes and socks (provide size)
  • Stationery or journals
  • Shower shoes
  • Games, cards, videos, audiobooks
