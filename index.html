Welcome

CSC is a non-partisan, non-endorsing 501(c)(3), that supplies care packages to any deployed servicemember as a means to boost morale and stave off suicide and suicidal thoughts. Care packages from home can mean a lot to someone, especially if that person does not have family at home, or the family is unable to send such items. Even just a card and a candy bar can make someone’s day. Many of these items are also a luxury in the field on deployment. CSC is fully volunteer, and 100% of donations go into our care packages. We have ZERO overhead expenses – our storage space is donated; our boxes are donated; our papers and postage are donated. Every dollar given goes into care packages for our servicemembers.
