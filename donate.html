With the help of some of our military members, we have created a challenge coin. On the coin, you will see our slogan " Powered by civilians, Supplied to our Heroes". It also has a bottle opener. That feature was something that our military members said they wished they had a bottle opener while out in the field. Like any non-profit, raising money is the hardest part. We are selling our coins. If you are local, and we can deliver to you, the cost will be $20.00 per coin. We will ship to you, $25.00 for the coin, which includes the shipping cost. All proceeds go back into the non-profit.

To donate, please contact us directly or visit our secure donation platform.

  • Protein and other granola bars
  • Beef jerky/beef sticks
