May- Donation Event- State College AAUW Book Donation

After the last day of the sale, unsold books are donated to CSC. This is usually a Tuesday evening event.

Volunteers are needed to help load, transport, and unload. Additional volunteers could be used after the donation to help pack the books to prepare for shipping.
